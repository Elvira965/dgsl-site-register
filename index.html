DGSL Handover Details

Checklist

1. Current approved drawings, specification, RFI responses, setting-out data and revisions available at workface.
2. Task-specific RAMS briefed; workers inducted; Safe Pass / CSCS / trade competence checked as applicable.
3. Work area, access, lighting, scaffold / edge protection, temporary works, previous trade and substrate accepted
4. Materials / products approved and traceable; plant, tools and test equipment inspected / certified / calibrated.
5. Interfaces with adjacent trades, services, deliveries, exclusion zones and shared access agreed.
6. Protection of completed work plus weather, water, dust, noise and environmental controls agreed.
7. Hold / Witness Points, first-off, photos, tests and QA records identified; emergency, waste, housekeeping and security controls agreed.
